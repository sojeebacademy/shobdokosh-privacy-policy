Privacy Policy

Introduction

Thank you for using Shobdokosh. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it when you use our Bangla Dictionary app.

Shobdokosh is a Bangla dictionary app. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data responsibly.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • App usage statistics (e.g. frequently searched words)
  • Device type and operating system version
  • Crash reports and error logs

Information We Do Not Collect

  • Your name or personal identity
  • Phone number or email address
  • Location or GPS data
  • Camera or microphone data
  • Financial or banking information
  • Contacts or messages

Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device only:

  • Bookmarked words
  • Search history
  • Theme and font preferences
  • App settings
This data never leaves your device and is not sent to any server.

Data Security

All dictionary data in this app is protected with AES-256-GCM encryption. We follow industry-standard security practices to protect your information.

Children's Privacy

Shobdokosh is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
